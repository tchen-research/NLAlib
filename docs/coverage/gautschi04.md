# Coverage of Orthogonal Polynomials: Computation and Approximation (`gautschi04`)

Walter Gautschi, Orthogonal Polynomials: Computation and Approximation, Oxford University Press (Numerical Mathematics and Scientific Computation), 2004; text extracted with pdftotext -layout, pages are the printed page numbers. Theorems, lemmas, definitions and examples share one counter per chapter (e.g. 1.10–1.15 are examples), so gaps in theorem numbers are examples; corollaries are unnumbered ('Corollary to Theorem 1.31', 'Corollary 1 to Theorem 2.22'). The book has no exercises. Examples (labelled 'Example n.m', not 'Ex', which this project reserves for exercises) are indexed only when they state a named classical result (1.49, 1.50, 3.46, 3.47); numbered Algorithms are indexed (kind 'algorithm'), and three unnumbered passages central to NLAlib are indexed by section (Sec 2.2.3.1 Stieltjes procedure, Sec 2.2.3.2 Lanczos-type algorithm, Sec 3.1.7 matrix functionals as integrals). Chapter 1 = basic theory, 2 = computational methods, 3 = applications. NLAlib's orthogonal-polynomial theory is stated for the spectral measure μ_b of a symmetric matrix (finitely supported), so results for general measures are at best 'partial'.

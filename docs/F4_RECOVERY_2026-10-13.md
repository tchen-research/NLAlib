# F4 compilation recovery checkpoint — 2026-10-13

The working tree on `nlalib-f4` now passes the full `lake build` (5,012 jobs,
`/tmp/f4-full-recovery4.log`). This is a compilation checkpoint, not completion of the
F4 consistency brief; the API and port-plan work below remains before the new library.
No commit, push, or PR was created in this continuation.

## What changed

The interrupted wave-1 APIs were repaired and propagated through Matrix, Probability,
RandomMatrix, Krylov, Polynomial, Estimation, Sketching, and LowRank. Solvers compiled
against those changes without source edits. The repairs preserve mathematical bounds
and did not add scaffolds, axioms, or `native_decide`.

- All Matrix areas now compile, including the standard extended-real condition number,
  named norms, indexed smallest singular values, Hermitian projectors, and generic frames.
- Gaussian and Rademacher concentration consumers use the new law and moment APIs.
  Inverse-Gram identities use explicit or almost-everywhere full-rank evidence.
- Krylov, random-start, Hutchinson, and SLQ proofs compile with the revised APIs.
- SRHT uses real Rademacher vectors. Deterministic sign matrices explicitly require
  sign-square support; random statements use that support almost everywhere. New reusable
  measurable-product and finite-support tail lemmas justify the conditioning step.
- LowRank consumes the revised best-approximation, norm, filter, and elimination APIs.
  Duplicate Cholesky probability and complex subspace-iterate definitions were removed
  in favor of their shared generic definitions.
- Audit module selections follow moved/merged modules and reject missing selections.
  Completed Cholesky bundle successors are selected by declaration because their shared
  module also contains two older pivoted-rank scaffolds outside that audit’s scope.
  The condition-number and random-start atlas prose was corrected, as were two stale
  coverage references. Source lint and import-layer checks were retained.

Detailed reports: [M1](f4/M1.md), [M2](f4/M2.md), [Probability/RandomMatrix](f4/P.md),
[algorithms/estimation](f4/A1.md), [Sketching](f4/Sketching_compile_recovery.md).
Rename recipes are in `docs/renames/2026-10-F4-*.json`, including the continuation and
Sketching maps. Builds used `/tmp/nlalib-f4-build.lock` to serialize Lean processes.

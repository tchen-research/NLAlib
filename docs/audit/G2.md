# Audit G2: sketching and low-rank approximation

Scope: atlas areas `sketching*`, `lra*` (28 results); modules `NLAlib/Sketching/**` (7 files) and
`NLAlib/LowRank/**` (11 files); the LRA project's `tgn-expected-error` and its hypotheses
(`~/Documents/Papers/LRA-paper/formal/LRA/Theorems/TruncatedGN.lean`, `LRA/Standard/Gaussian.lean`,
`tGN_replacement.tex`). Read-only audit, 2026-10-10.

Reuse counts are `(direct in-edges / transitive in-edges)` in `atlas.json` `depends_on`.

## Headline findings

1. **G2 needs almost no new assumptions.** The current `assumed`/`scaffold` nodes in G2
   (`jl-distributional`, `hmt-9-1-spectral`, and the `VonNeumannRankBound` hypothesis of
   `tgn-expected-error`) can all be proved from the existing library at difficulty 2, with no new
   assumptions. Only two items below justify a scaffold: the packaged sampling form of matrix
   Chernoff and HMT Prop 8.6 (power iteration).
2. **`jl-distributional` is not discharged by the existing files as they stand, but nothing new
   needs to be assumed to finish it.** Exactly what is missing (details in C1):
   - The *public* chi-square lower tail `measure_sum_sq_le_le_gaussianReal`,
     `P(χ²_d ≤ u) ≤ (e u/d)^{d/2}`, is too weak for JL. At `u = (1−ε)d` the base is `e(1−ε)`,
     which is `> 1` for `ε < 1 − 1/e`, so the bound is useless. The sharp Chernoff form is
     already proved, but in the *private* lemma `measureReal_sum_sq_le_le_exp_mul`
     (`ChiSquare.lean:103`). That lemma needs to be exposed, or a sharp-form corollary added.
   - The chi-square **upper** tail is not proved (atlas `chi-square-upper-tail` is `candidate`,
     not proved as the task brief assumed). It follows from the public Laplace transform
     `integral_exp_neg_mul_sum_sq_pi_gaussianReal`, which holds for every `u > 0` and so gives
     the MGF at every `t < 1/2`, together with Mathlib's
     `ProbabilityTheory.measure_ge_le_exp_mul_mgf`. Integrability comes from
     `MeasureTheory.Integrable.of_integral_ne_zero`.
   - Rotation invariance `gaussianMatrix_map_orthogonal` is for `U G V` with square orthogonal
     `U`, `V`. JL needs the law of `G x` for a unit vector `x`, which is the *right* action on a
     column. NLAlib has the left-block law `gaussianMatrix_map_block`, but no transpose or
     right-multiplication law (`grep` finds none).
   - Two scalar inequalities, neither in Mathlib: `log(1+ε) ≤ ε − ε²/2 + ε³/3` and
     `log(1−ε) ≤ −ε − ε²/2`.
   - The `jl_distributional` docstring is out of date: it says rotation invariance and the
     chi-square tails are "not yet in the library".
3. **The atlas statement of `tgn-expected-error` does not match the formal theorem it cites.**
   See C-flags.
4. **Layering defect.** Hanson–Wright lives in `NLAlib/Estimation/HansonWright.lean` (layer 4),
   so `Sketching` (layer 3) cannot use it, which blocks `jl-subgaussian`. Move it to
   `Concentration/Scalar` (layer 1), its atlas area.

  `block-krylov-lra` is only `O(·)`-stated.
- `etrsvd`, `etgn`, early-truncation `truncation-lemma`: deferred at the user's request (atlas
  notes). Excluded.

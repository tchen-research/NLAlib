# Targeting round 7, group T1: Krylov methods beyond CG

25 candidates, all `status: candidate`, ready to merge from `docs/targets/T1.json`. Five new
sub-areas sit under `krylov`: `krylov-bases`, `krylov-linear-systems`, `krylov-nonnormal`,
`krylov-matrix-functions` and `krylov-eigenvalues`. One candidate goes in the existing
`polynomial-approximation` area and one in `lra-deterministic`. There are 16 new sources.

Statements use real matrices with `Fintype` indices unless they are intrinsically complex (field
of values, Crouzeix–Palencia, BGT, GPS, Arnoldi-FA). Real GMRES loses nothing for real data: when
`r₀` is real, replacing a complex residual polynomial by its real-coefficient part never
increases `‖p(A)r₀‖`. So bounds over complex spectra and complex sets apply to the real
definition.

## What NLAlib already has (checked in `atlas/declarations.json`)

- `krylovSpace` (real only), with its polynomial characterisation, nesting, `A`-invariance into
  the next space and `finrank ≤ q`.
- `lanczosMatrix`, the Gram–Schmidt basis of `b, Ab, …, A^{q-1}b`. `range = K_q` and orthonormal
  columns under linear independence are proved. **`lanczos_apply_eq_zero_of_add_one_lt` proves
  that `QᵀAQ` is upper Hessenberg for every real `A`**, so `lanczosMatrix` is already the Arnoldi
  basis. Tridiagonality is proved for symmetric `A`. The recurrence identity itself is not stated.
- `mulVec_pow_transpose_mul_mul_mulVec_eq`: `Q (QᵀAQ)^j Qᵀ b = A^j b` for `j < q`, for any real
  `A` and any orthonormal `Q` with `K_q ⊆ range Q`. This is the power form of Saad 1992 Lemma 3.1.
- `eigenvalues_transpose_mul_mul_mem_Icc`: Ritz values lie in any interval containing the spectrum.
- `abs_dotProduct_cfc_mulVec_sub_le_of_krylovSpace_le`: the quadratic-form, i.e. quadrature,
  version of the Lanczos-FA bound.
- `IsCGIterate` (variational), `exists_isCGIterate`, and the CG κ-bound in general-polynomial and
  Chebyshev forms. The affine-Krylov ↔ residual-polynomial step is a **private** lemma
  (`exists_degree_lt_one_sub_eq_X_mul`) in `NLAlib/Krylov/CG.lean`.
- `ritz-value-bounds`: the *gap-free* Ritz bound via the Chebyshev amplifier. The atlas entry
  cites `saad11` Thm 6.4, but the gap-dependent Kaniel–Paige–Saad statement under that label is
  not formalised.
- The polynomial layer: `chebyshevResidual`, `isLeast_chebyshev_minimax`, `chebyshevAmplifier`,
  `chebyshev-growth` and `polynomial-spectral-bound` (`specNorm_aeval_le`,
  `dotProduct_aeval_mulVec_self_le`, `quadForm_aeval_mulVec_le`).
- `hmt-9-1-spectral` and `hmt-9-1-frobenius` (proved), and `power-iteration-deterministic`.

## The five to do first

1. **`residual-polynomial`** (difficulty 1). This candidate has the highest in-degree in the
   proposal. It promotes the private CG lemma to a public statement and adds `ε_k(S)`, which
   every polynomial method then states its rate with. The interval value is the existing
   `isLeast_chebyshev_minimax`.
2. **`gmres-def`** (difficulty 2). It is the variational definition and copies the `IsCGIterate`
   pattern. Mathlib's Hilbert projection theorems give existence and the Petrov–Galerkin
   characterisation almost for free. Six candidates depend on it, and so does sketched GMRES.
3. **`arnoldi-relation`**, together with its naming step **`arnoldi-def`** (difficulty 2 each).
   `lanczosMatrix` is already the Arnoldi basis and already Hessenberg for every `A`. The column
   identity `AQ_q = Q_qH_q + h_{q+1,q}q_{q+1}e_qᵀ` is a short expansion in an orthonormal basis.
   It closes the open half of `lanczos-recurrence`, a batch-D target.
4. **`lanczos-fa-error`** (difficulty 2). It is the vector analogue of the proved quadrature
   error and uses only lemmas NLAlib already has. With `inverse-polynomial-approx`,
   `bernstein-ellipse-approx` and T2's approximations, it turns every polynomial-approximation
   rate into an f(A)b guarantee.
5. **`preconditioned-cg`** (difficulty 2). Stated under a Loewner sandwich `aP ≼ A ≼ bP`, which
   is exactly how randomized preconditioners are certified. It is the missing deterministic link
   for `nystrom-pcg`. `cgls-lsqr-bound` (difficulty 1) plays the same role for
   `sketch-and-precondition` and can go in the same session.

Runner-up, also `now`: **`krylov-eigenvector-angle`**. It is the gap-dependent Chebyshev core
behind Kaniel–Paige–Saad, random-start Lanczos and gap-dependent block Krylov.

## Candidates in detail

Each entry gives: the statement; its source; what Mathlib has or lacks; what NLAlib already has;
consumers; difficulty; and whether to prove or assume. The JSON holds the formal
hypothesis/conclusion split.

### A. Krylov bases (`krylov-bases`)

#### 1. `krylov-grade`: grade of a vector (theorem, difficulty 2, prove)

**Statement.** Let `ν = deg μ_{A,b}`, where `μ_{A,b}` is the monic polynomial of least degree with
`μ_{A,b}(A)b = 0`. Then:
- `dim K_q(A,b) = min(q, ν)`;
- `b, …, A^{q-1}b` are independent iff `q ≤ ν`;
- `K_ν` is `A`-invariant and `K_q = K_ν` for `q ≥ ν`;
- `μ_{A,b} ∣ μ_A`;
- if `A` is invertible, then `A⁻¹b ∈ K_ν`.

**Source.** Saad 2003 Props 6.1–6.3; Liesen–Strakoš §2.2.

**Mathlib.** It has `Module.AEval'`: `K_∞` is the cyclic `K[X]`-submodule generated by `b`. It has
`annIdealGenerator` (for algebra elements, not vectors), `minpoly` and Cayley–Hamilton. It lacks
a vector-relative minimal polynomial.

**NLAlib.** `krylovSpace` and `finrank_krylovSpace_le`.

**Consumers.**
- Finite termination for GMRES and CG.
- Arnoldi breakdown.
- The `LinearIndependent` non-breakdown hypothesis of `hasOrthonormalCols_lanczosMatrix`, which
  is exactly `q ≤ ν`.

**API note.** Generalising `krylovSpace` from ℝ to a field (or `RCLike`) is a prerequisite for
the complex entries. It refactors the existing `krylov-subspace`, so it is not a new atlas id.

#### 2. `arnoldi-def`: Arnoldi basis and Hessenberg matrices (definition, difficulty 2, prove)

**Definitions.**
- `Q_q` = Gram–Schmidt of the Krylov vectors.
- `H_q = Q_qᵀAQ_q`.
- `H̲_q = Q_{q+1}ᵀAQ_q`.
- A matrix is upper Hessenberg if `h_ij = 0` for `i > j+1`.

**Characterisations.**
- Orthonormal columns spanning `K_q` when `q ≤ ν`.
- `H_q` and `H̲_q` are upper Hessenberg.
- `h_{j+1,j} = ‖(I − Q_jQ_jᵀ)Aq_j‖`, which is `> 0` iff `j < ν`.
- Implicit-Q uniqueness: an orthonormal `Q̃` with `Q̃e₁ = b/‖b‖` and `Q̃ᵀAQ̃` Hessenberg with a
  positive subdiagonal equals `Q_q`.

**Source.** Saad 2003 §6.3, Alg. 6.1, Prop. 6.4; Trefethen–Bau Lect. 33.

**Mathlib.** Gram–Schmidt is present. It has no Hessenberg predicate.

**NLAlib.** Almost everything: `lanczosMatrix` and the Hessenberg lemma for general `A`. The
entry is mostly naming plus the subdiagonal and uniqueness facts.

#### 3. `arnoldi-relation` (identity, difficulty 2, prove)

**Statement.**
- `AQ_q = Q_qH_q + h_{q+1,q} q_{q+1} e_qᵀ = Q_{q+1}H̲_q`.
- `h_{q+1,q} = 0` iff `q = ν`, and then `AQ_q = Q_qH_q`.
- For symmetric `A`, `H_q = T_q` is tridiagonal, giving the three-term recurrence
  `Aq_j = β_{j-1}q_{j-1} + α_jq_j + β_jq_{j+1}`.

**Source.** Saad 2003 Props 6.5–6.6; Trefethen–Bau Lect. 33/36; Golub–Meurant Thm 4.2.

**Consumers.**
- Existing `lanczos-recurrence`, which should gain `depends_on: arnoldi-relation`.
- FOM.
- The GMRES least-squares form.
- Arnoldi-FA.

#### 4. `krylov-polynomial-exactness` (identity, difficulty 2, prove)

**Statement.** For any `A` and orthonormal `Q` with `K_q ⊆ range Q`, and `H = QᵀAQ`:
- `p(A)b = Q p(H) Qᵀb` when `deg p < q`;
- `Qᵀp(A)b = p(H)Qᵀb` when `deg p ≤ q`;
- if `range Q = K_q` with `q ≤ ν`, then `χ_H` uniquely minimises `‖p(A)b‖` over monic `p` of
  degree `q`. This is the Arnoldi/Lanczos eigenvalue characterisation.

**Source.** Saad 1992 Lemma 3.1; Trefethen–Bau Thm 34.1.

**Mathlib.** Cayley–Hamilton and the projection characterisation.

**NLAlib.** The power form is already a helper (`mulVec_pow_transpose_mul_mul_mulVec_eq`).

**Consumers.**
- `lanczos-fa-error`.
- `arnoldi-fa-error`.
- `fom-def`.
- A refactor of `lanczos-gauss-quadrature`.

#### 5. `block-krylov-subspace` (definition, difficulty 1, prove)

**Statement.**
`K_q(A,Ω) = range[Ω, AΩ, …, A^{q-1}Ω] = Σ_j K_q(A, ω_j) = {Σ_{i<q} A^iΩC_i}`. It contains
`range p(A)Ω` for `deg p < q`. It is nested in `q`, satisfies `A K_q ⊆ K_{q+1}`, and has
dimension `≤ qs`. The rectangular form is `K_q(AAᵀ, AΩ) ∋ φ(AAᵀ)AΩ`.

**Source.** Musco–Musco 2015 Alg. 2; Tropp–Webber §7; Saad 2011 §6.3.

**Lean shape.** Define it as `⨆ j, krylovSpace A (Ω · j) q` so single-vector lemmas transfer.

**Consumers.**
- `polynomial-filter-range-finder`.
- `block-krylov-lra`.
- `subspace-iteration-expected`.
- Block-Krylov trace estimation.

### B. Polynomial methods for linear systems (`krylov-linear-systems`)

#### 6. `residual-polynomial`: `P_k^0` and `ε_k(S)` (definition, difficulty 1, prove)

**Definitions.** `P_k^0 = {p : deg p ≤ k, p(0) = 1}` and
`ε_k(S) = inf_{p∈P_k^0} sup_{z∈S} |p(z)|`.

**Characterisations.**
- `x ∈ x₀ + K_k(A, r₀)` iff `b − Ax = p(A)r₀` for some `p ∈ P_k^0`.
- `ε_k` is antitone in `k` and monotone in `S`.
- The infimum is attained for finite `S`.
- `ε_k([a,b]) = 1/T_k((b+a)/(b−a))`.

**Source.** Saad 2003 §6.11; Trefethen–Bau Lect. 35; Greenbaum Ch. 3.

**NLAlib.** The CG private lemma and `isLeast_chebyshev_minimax`.

**Consumers.** Nine in this proposal, plus `cg-convergence`.

#### 7. `gmres-def`: GMRES iterate (definition, difficulty 2, prove)

**Definition.** `x_k ∈ x₀ + K_k(A, r₀)` minimising `‖b − Ax‖₂` over that affine space.

**Characterisations.**
- An iterate always exists.
- `r_k` is unique, and `x_k` is unique if `A` is invertible.
- `r_k ⟂ A K_k`.
- `‖r_k‖ = min_{P_k^0} ‖p(A)r₀‖`.
- Residuals are monotone.
- `‖r_k‖ = min_y ‖ ‖r₀‖e₁ − H̲_k y ‖`.
- `r_ν = 0` for invertible `A`.
- For symmetric `A`, this is MINRES.

**Source.** Saad–Schultz 1986 §2–3; Saad 2003 §6.5; Trefethen–Bau Lect. 35; Paige–Saunders 1975.

**Mathlib.** `Submodule.exists_norm_eq_iInf_of_complete_subspace`,
`Submodule.norm_eq_iInf_iff_real_inner_eq_zero`, `Submodule.starProjection_minimal`.

**Consumers.**
- All GMRES/MINRES bounds.
- Restarted GMRES.
- Sketched GMRES (Nakatsukasa–Tropp 2024), which is a sketch-and-solve consumer together with
  `sketch-and-solve-ls`.

#### 8. `gmres-diagonalizable` (bound, difficulty 2, prove)

**Statement.** If `A = V diag(λ) V⁻¹` over ℂ, then
`‖r_k‖ ≤ ‖V‖‖V⁻¹‖ · max_i |p(λ_i)| · ‖r₀‖` for every real `p ∈ P_k^0`. For normal `A` the factor
is 1.

**Source.** Saad–Schultz 1986 §3.4; Saad 2003 Prop. 6.32; Trefethen–Bau Thm 35.2.

**Mathlib.** `Matrix.l2_opNorm_mul` and `Matrix.l2_opNorm_mulVec` are present. It lacks a
diagonalisability predicate, so the statement uses explicit `V` and `Λ`.

**Sharp form.** Worst-case sharpness for normal `A` (Greenbaum–Gurvits 1994): the maximum of
`‖r_k‖` over unit `r₀` equals `ε_k(σ(A))`. This is a later variant at difficulty 4, to be assumed.

#### 9. `gmres-elman` (bound, difficulty 2, prove)

**Statement.** Let `M = (A+Aᵀ)/2 ≻ 0`, `α = λ_min(M)` and `β = ‖A‖`. Then
`‖r_k‖ ≤ (1 − α²/β²)^{k/2} ‖r₀‖`. GMRES(m) after `j` cycles inherits this as
`(1 − α²/β²)^{jm/2}`. This entry absorbs restarted GMRES.

**Source.** Eisenstat–Elman–Schultz 1983 Thm 3.3; Saad 2003 §6.11.4.

**Proof.** The single step `‖(I − ωA)r‖² ≤ (1 − 2ωα + ω²β²)‖r‖²` with `ω = α/β²`. The argument is
entirely real.

**Variant.** A complex form with `dist(0, W(A))`.

#### 10. `minres-bound` (bound, difficulty 2, prove)

**Statement.** For symmetric invertible `A`:
- `‖r_k‖ ≤ max_i |p(λ_i)| ‖r₀‖` for every `p ∈ P_k^0`;
- on `[a,b] ∪ [c,d]` with `a < b < 0 < c < d` and `d−c = b−a`:
  `‖r_{2k}‖ ≤ 2((√|ad| − √|bc|)/(√|ad| + √|bc|))^k ‖r₀‖`;
- for `[−d,−c] ∪ [c,d]`: `2((κ−1)/(κ+1))^k`;
- definite case: `2((√b−√a)/(√b+√a))^k`.

**Source.** Greenbaum 1997 §3.1; Paige–Saunders 1975; Liesen–Strakoš §5.6.

**Consumers.**
- Saddle-point / KKT solves.
- LSMR inside sketch-and-precondition.
- Indefinite Newton–sketch steps.

#### 11. `fom-def`: FOM iterate (definition, difficulty 2, prove, later)

**Definition.** `x ∈ x₀ + K_k` with `r ⟂ K_k`.

**Characterisations.**
- An iterate exists uniquely iff `H_k` is invertible.
- `x_k^F = x₀ + ‖r₀‖ Q_k H_k⁻¹ e₁`.
- `‖r_k^F‖ = h_{k+1,k} |e_kᵀH_k⁻¹e₁| ‖r₀‖`.
- For SPD `A`, the FOM iterate is the CG iterate (`IsCGIterate`).

**Source.** Saad 2003 §6.4.1; Saad 1992 §3.

**Later variant.** The Brown / Cullum–Greenbaum peak–plateau relation
`‖r^F_k‖ = ‖r^G_k‖/√(1 − (‖r^G_k‖/‖r^G_{k−1}‖)²)` (Saad §6.5.7).

**Role.** FOM is Arnoldi-FA for `f = 1/z`.

#### 12. `chebyshev-iteration` (theorem, difficulty 3, prove, later)

**Statement.** Take the three-term recurrence of Saad Alg. 12.1 (`θ`, `δ`, `σ`, `ρ_k`, `d_k`). It
produces `r_k = R_k(A)r₀` with `R_k = chebyshevResidual a b k`. Hence
`‖r_k‖ ≤ 2((√b−√a)/(√b+√a))^k ‖r₀‖`, and the same in the `A`-norm. No inner products are needed.

**Source.** Saad 2003 §12.3, Alg. 12.1; Golub–Varga 1961.

**Mathlib.** `Polynomial.Chebyshev.T_add_two`.

**Consumers.**
- LSRN (Chebyshev semi-iteration on sketch-preconditioned normal equations).
- Chebyshev filtering.
- Kernel polynomial method.

#### 13. `richardson-iteration` (bound, difficulty 2, prove)

**Statement.** Let `A, P ≻ 0` with `aP ≼ A ≼ bP`, and iterate `x_{k+1} = x_k + ωP⁻¹(c − Ax_k)`.
Then `e_k = (I − ωP⁻¹A)^k e₀` and
`‖e_k‖_A ≤ max(|1−ωa|, |1−ωb|)^k ‖e₀‖_A`, with factor `(b−a)/(b+a)` at `ω = 2/(a+b)`.

**Source.** Greenbaum §2.1; Saad 2003 §4.2.

**Consumers.**
- Iterative sketching for least squares (Pilanci–Wainwright IHS; Epperly 2024), with
  `P = (SA)ᵀSA` and `a, b = (1 ± ε)^{−2}` from `ose-def`.
- Sketch-and-project read as preconditioned Richardson.

**Later variant.** Heavy-ball momentum.

#### 14. `cgls-lsqr-bound` (bound, difficulty 1, prove)

**Statement.** Let `A` have full column rank with singular values in `[σ_lo, σ_hi]`, and let
`x_k` minimise `‖b − Ax‖` over `x₀ + K_k(AᵀA, Aᵀr₀)`. Then:
- `‖A(x_k − x⋆)‖ ≤ 2((σ_hi−σ_lo)/(σ_hi+σ_lo))^k ‖A(x₀ − x⋆)‖`;
- `‖b−Ax_k‖² = ‖b−Ax⋆‖² + ‖A(x_k−x⋆)‖²`.

**Source.** Saad 2003 §8.3 (CGNR); Paige–Saunders 1982.

**Proof.** It is literally `quadForm_sub_le_of_isCGIterate` at `AᵀA`.

**Consumer.** `sketch-and-precondition` (Blendenpik, LSRN): with `κ(AR⁻¹) ≤ (1+ε)/(1−ε)`, the
end-to-end iteration count follows.

#### 15. `preconditioned-cg` (bound, difficulty 2, prove)

**Statement.** Let `A, P ≻ 0` with `aP ≼ A ≼ bP`, and let the PCG iterate minimise the `A`-norm
error over `x₀ + K_k(P⁻¹A, P⁻¹r₀)`. Then
`‖x_k − x⋆‖_A ≤ 2((√b−√a)/(√b+√a))^k ‖x₀ − x⋆‖_A`. The reduction to CG goes through `P = LLᵀ`:
`K_k(P⁻¹A, P⁻¹r₀) = L^{-ᵀ}K_k(L⁻¹AL^{-ᵀ}, L⁻¹r₀)`.

**Source.** Saad 2003 §9.2; Greenbaum Ch. 8.

**Mathlib.** `LDL.lower_conj_diag`, `CFC.sqrt`, `Matrix.PosDef`.

**Consumers.**
- `nystrom-pcg` (Frangella–Tropp–Udell).
- RPCholesky preconditioning.

### C. Field of values and non-normal GMRES (`krylov-nonnormal`)

#### 16. `field-of-values-def` (definition, difficulty 3, prove)

**Definition.** `W(A) = {x*Ax : ‖x‖ = 1} ⊂ ℂ`. For real `A`, take the complexification.

**Characterisations.**
- Compact.
- Contains `σ(A)`.
- Contained in `B̄(0, ‖A‖)`.
- Affine covariance.
- Unitary invariance.
- **`W(Q*AQ) ⊆ W(A)` for orthonormal `Q`**, which is the key fact for Arnoldi `H_q`.
- `Re W(A) = [λ_min(M), λ_max(M)]`.
- Convex (Toeplitz–Hausdorff, the only difficulty-3 piece and not needed by the assumed
  consumers).
- `W(A) = conv σ(A)` for normal `A`.

**Source.** Horn–Johnson *Topics* Ch. 1; Greenbaum §3.2.

**Mathlib.** Nothing.

#### 17. `crouzeix-palencia` (theorem, difficulty 5, **assume**)

**Statement.** `‖p(A)‖₂ ≤ (1+√2) max_{W(A)} |p|` for every polynomial `p`. Hence GMRES satisfies
`‖r_k‖ ≤ (1+√2) ε_k(W(A)) ‖r₀‖`.

**Source.** Crouzeix–Palencia 2017.

**Why assume.** Stating it for polynomials avoids the missing holomorphic functional calculus.
The statement is certain and short. The proof (Cauchy transform on the boundary of a convex set)
is research-level to formalise.

#### 18. `gmres-bgt` (bound, difficulty 4, **assume**, later)

**Statement.** Suppose `0 ∉ W(A)` and `cos β = dist(0, W(A))/‖A‖`. Then
`‖r_k‖ ≤ (2 + 2/√3)(2 + γ_β) γ_β^k ‖r₀‖`, where `γ_β = 2 sin(β/(4 − 2β/π)) < sin β`.

**Source.** Beckermann–Goreinov–Tyrtyshnikov 2006.

**Proof dependencies.** The constant comes from a lens-shaped von Neumann-type inequality
(Beckermann–Crouzeix), not from Crouzeix–Palencia, so a proof would need that inequality plus a
Faber construction.

#### 19. `gmres-any-convergence-curve` (theorem, difficulty 3, prove, later)

**Statement.** Take any nonincreasing positive `f(0..n−1)` and any nonzero `λ_1..λ_n`. Then some
`A` with that spectrum and some `b` have GMRES residual norms exactly `f(k)`.

**Source.** Greenbaum–Pták–Strakoš 1996; Liesen–Strakoš §5.7.

**Mathlib.** It lacks a companion-matrix API.

**Role.** A caution with no consumers. It explains the `κ(V)` in entry 8 and why field-of-values
bounds are needed.

### D. Krylov approximation of f(A)b (`krylov-matrix-functions`)

#### 20. `lanczos-fa-error` (bound, difficulty 2, prove)

**Statement.** Let `A` be symmetric with spectrum in `[λ_lo, λ_hi]`, `Q` orthonormal with
`K_q ⊆ range Q`, and `T = QᵀAQ`. If `deg p < q` and `|f − p| ≤ E` on the interval, then
`‖cfc f A · b − Q cfc f T · Qᵀb‖ ≤ 2E‖b‖`. For Lanczos, `Qᵀb = ‖b‖e₁`.

**Source.** Druskin–Knizhnerman 1989; Higham 2008 §13.2; Saad 1992 Lemma 3.1.

**Proof.** Exactness, then two spectral bounds. The Ritz values are already in the interval.

**Consumers.**
- Krylov-aware trace estimation of `f(A)`.
- Gaussian sampling via `A^{1/2}b`.
- Exponential actions.
- Block-Lanczos forms of `slq-error`.

#### 21. `arnoldi-fa-error` (bound, difficulty 3, prove modulo the assumed CP, later)

**Statement.** For any complex `A`, orthonormal `Q ⊇ K_q`, `H = Q*AQ`, any polynomial `g`, and
`p` with `deg p < q` and `|g − p| ≤ E` on `W(A)`:
`‖g(A)b − Q g(H) Q*b‖ ≤ 2(1+√2) E ‖b‖`.

**Source.** Beckermann–Reichel 2009 §2; Higham §13.2; Crouzeix–Palencia 2017.

**Why `g` is a polynomial.** Mathlib has no Riesz–Dunford calculus for non-normal matrices, so
`g` must be a polynomial. `NormedSpace.exp` reaches `exp` by truncation.

### E. Ritz values and eigenvector angles (`krylov-eigenvalues`)

#### 22. `krylov-eigenvector-angle` (bound, difficulty 2, prove)

**Statement.** Assume `λ₁ > λ₂ > λ_n` and `⟨u₁, b⟩ ≠ 0`. Then
`x = T_{q−1}((2A − (λ₂+λ_n)I)/(λ₂−λ_n)) b ∈ K_q` has
`tan∠(u₁, x) ≤ tan∠(u₁, b) / T_{q−1}(1+2γ)`, where `γ = (λ₁−λ₂)/(λ₂−λ_n)`.

**Source.** Saad 2011 §6.6.1; Saad 2003 §6.11.

**Builds on.** `polynomial-spectral-bound` and `chebyshev-growth`.

**Consumers.**
- KPS.
- `random-start-power`: with a Gaussian start, `tan²∠(u₁, g)` is a ratio of chi-squares.
- Gap-dependent `block-krylov-lra` and `subspace-iteration-expected`.
- PCA eigenvector guarantees.

**Later variant.** The general `u_i` form with factor `κ_i`.

#### 23. `kaniel-paige-saad` (bound, difficulty 2, prove)

**Statement.** `0 ≤ λ₁ − θ₁ ≤ (λ₁ − λ_n) tan²∠(u₁, b) / T_{q−1}(1+2γ)²`.

**Source.** Saad 2011 Thm 6.4; Golub–Meurant Ch. 4.

**Relation to the existing entry.** This is the statement that `ritz-value-bounds`' citation
"saad11 Thm 6.4" actually refers to. It is a variant of that entry, not a duplicate: gap-dependent
rather than gap-free, and with a `tan²` start dependence. The general `i`-th Ritz value form is
later.

### F. Elsewhere

#### 24. `two-interval-minimax` (`polynomial-approximation`; bound, difficulty 1, prove)

**Statement.** Take `a<b<0<c<d` with `d−c = b−a`. Then
`p(x) = R_k(x² − (b+c)x)`, with `R_k` = `chebyshevResidual |bc| |ad| k`, has `deg p ≤ 2k` and
`p(0) = 1`. On `[a,b] ∪ [c,d]` it satisfies
`|p| ≤ 2((√|ad|−√|bc|)/(√|ad|+√|bc|))^k`.

**Check.** The quadratic maps both intervals onto `[|bc|, |ad|]`; the identity
`(d−b)(d−c) − bc = |ad|` uses `d−c = b−a`.

**Source.** Greenbaum §3.1; Liesen–Strakoš §5.6.

**Merge note.** T2 may list indefinite approximation too; deduplicate on merge.

#### 25. `polynomial-filter-range-finder` (`lra-deterministic`; bound, difficulty 2, prove)

This is the **deterministic polynomial step of Musco–Musco**.

**Statement.** Take a block SVD at index `k` with `[V₁ V₂]` orthogonal, `Ω₁ = V₁ᵀΩ` of full row
rank, and `φ(Σ₁²)` invertible. Let `range(φ(AAᵀ)AΩ) ⊆ range Q`. Then
`‖(I−QQᵀ)A‖_ξ² ≤ ‖Σ₂‖_ξ² + ‖Σ₂ φ(Σ₂ᵀΣ₂) Ω₂ Ω₁† φ(Σ₁²)⁻¹‖_ξ²` for `ξ ∈ {2, F}`. Hence
`‖(I−QQᵀ)A‖² ≤ σ_{k+1}² + (max_{j>k} σ_j|φ(σ_j²)| / min_{j≤k} |φ(σ_j²)|)² ‖Ω₂‖²‖Ω₁†‖²`.

**Proof.** `Aφ(AᵀA) = φ(AAᵀ)A`, so `Y = AΩ̃` with `Ω̃ = φ(AᵀA)Ω`; then apply the proved HMT 9.1.

**Why it fits block Krylov.** `φ(AAᵀ)AΩ ∈ K_q(AAᵀ, AΩ)` for `deg φ < q`, so the bound holds for
the block Krylov basis with every such `φ` at once.

**Special cases.**
- `φ = x^q` recovers the power scheme.
- `φ` = the Chebyshev amplifier in `x²` gives the `√γ`-accelerated gap-dependent block Krylov
  rate.

**What remains for `block-krylov-lra`.** The gap-free spectral clause of Musco–Musco Thm 1 also
needs their per-vector Rayleigh–Ritz argument. That stays in the existing application entry.

**Source.** HMT 2011 Thm 9.1 and §9.2; Musco–Musco §4; Tropp–Webber §7.

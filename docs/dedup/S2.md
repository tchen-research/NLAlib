## Phase 4: namespace flattening

The flattening is one scripted rewrite: a Lean-4 name-resolution simulator and one rename map, regenerated after phase 3 from `atlas/declarations.json` together with the declarations parsed from source.
- **Scope tracking.** The script follows `namespace`/`section`/`end` and every `open`, including multi-line `open` commands.
- **Identifier rewriting.**
  - Every identifier in code, and in backticks in comments, is resolved under the old scopes.
  - Identifiers that resolve to a renamed declaration are rewritten to the shortest form that resolves uniquely under the new scopes.
  - Declaration sites are rewritten relative to the new namespace.
- **Command lines.** `namespace`/`end` lines are mapped, and dropped when the namespace merges into its parent. `open` lines are mapped, de-duplicated and dropped when redundant.
- **Safety checks.**
  - A local-binder heuristic protects bound variables.
  - A capture check flags identifiers that a new `SparseSketch.*` name would shadow. It found 0 ambiguities and 0 captures.
  - The map has 0 collisions and 0 clashes with existing `NLAlib.*` names.
- **First build.** The whole tree compiled on the first build after the rewrite.

**Namespaces.** The 48 `NLAlib.SparseFock.*` module namespaces become:
- `NLAlib.SparseSketch`;
- the sector namespaces `.UniformSparse`, `.Directional`, `.Heavy`, `.Light` and `.Mixed`;
- the type namespaces `FiniteLaw`, `Frame`, `Pattern`, `Level`, `Word`, `Leg`, `Profile`, `WeightedONBasis` and `HasLocalDegree`.

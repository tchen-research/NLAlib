## A. How the library is organised

### A.1 Layers and tree

One Lake package, one flat `namespace NLAlib` (plus `_root_.Matrix.IsHermitian.*` /
`PosSemidef.*` / `PosDef.*` theorems declared inside NLAlib modules for dot notation, and the two
global `Matrix` measurability instances in ForMathlib). Every `<Dir>.lean` is a generated
aggregator (`scripts/reorg.py --gen-aggregators`); content lives in leaf directories. A module may
import only its own layer or lower (`scripts/check_layers.py`):

### A.2 Vocabulary (STANDARDS §3, after F1)

- Field: the Matrix layer's core is stated over `[RCLike 𝕜]` with `conjTranspose`
  (`HasOrthonormalCols Q := Qᴴ * Q = 1`, `quadForm A z := star z ⬝ᵥ (A *ᵥ z)`, `frobInner` = real
  part, `specNorm`/`frobNorm`/`frobSq`, Loewner `≤` under `open scoped MatrixOrder`). Real users
  rewrite `ᴴ` to `ᵀ` with `Matrix.conjTranspose_eq_transpose_of_trivial`; transpose twins were
  deleted (37 deprecated aliases remain for names used outside the core).
- Index types: the core is `Fintype`-generic. `IsSVD A U V` is the sorted-index shape
  (`U : Matrix m (Fin p) 𝕜`, `V : Matrix n (Fin q) 𝕜`, `rectDiag` positional on `Fin p × Fin q`,
  `exists_isSVD` with auto-params `card m = p`, `card n = q`). `singularValues A : ℕ → ℝ` is
  0-indexed, sorted, zero-padded.
- Smallest eigenvalue: Mathlib's `hA.eigenvalues₀ ⟨card n - 1, _⟩` (`lamMin`/`sigmaMinSq` deleted).
- Probability (F2): `Matrix.instMeasurableSpace`/`instBorelSpace` are global instances in
  `ForMathlib/MeasureTheory/Matrix.lean`; `gaussianMatrix m n : Measure (Matrix m n ℝ)`;
  `HasLaw X (gaussianMatrix m n) μ` is the single law interface; tails are stated with `μ.real`.
- Algorithms (`docs/ALGORITHM_CONVENTIONS.md`, `docs/KRYLOV_DEFINITIONS.md`): exact-arithmetic
  recursions with `…Succeeds` predicates; Krylov iterates are predicates (`IsCGIterate`), not
  functions; Krylov is real by design.

## C. What an outside reader would find weird (ranked)

Ranked by how likely a numerical analyst or Mathlib reviewer is to notice. Pointers are to the
detailed reviews (M = matrix, A = algorithms, P = probability, I = infrastructure, with the item
number there). "Port" means FOUNDATIONS §4 defers it to `NLA-lib/NLAlib`.

### C.1 Definitions

1. **`conditionNumber A := specNorm A * specNorm (moorePenroseInverse A)`** gives κ(0) = 0 and
   σ₁/σ_r for rank-deficient `A`, where the textbooks say ∞. Rename (`pinvConditionNumber`) or make
   it `ℝ≥0∞`-valued. (M2)
2. **Two different norms print as `‖A‖`.** `frobNorm_eq_norm : frobNorm A = ‖A‖` and
   `specNorm_eq_norm : specNorm A = ‖A‖` look contradictory because the scoped instance is invisible
   in a signature; about 110 Matrix statements, including core files, still use raw `‖·‖`. Rule:
   matrix norms in statements are always `specNorm`/`frobNorm`; lint it. (M1, A §3)
3. **Three notions of "smallest singular/eigen-value".** `sigmaMin` (ℝ-only `⨅`, 0 for every wide
   matrix, never identified with `singularValues`), `lambdaMin`/`lambdaMax` (ℂ-only `sInf (spectrum ℝ A)`,
   junk for non-Hermitian), and Mathlib's `eigenvalues₀ ⟨card n - 1, _⟩` (the official one). Two
   theorems are still named `lamMin_*` after the deleted definition. (M3)
4. **Singular values are 0-indexed and zero-padded**, so textbook σ_{k+1} is `singularValues A k`;
   `IsSVD` is the full SVD only (no thin SVD, which HMT use); `exists_isSVD` shows an auto-param in
   its signature. Intentional; document the off-by-one in the atlas and add `IsThinSVD` at port.
   (M4, A §2.4)
5. **Junk-value "equalities" carry atlas tags.** `specNorm (A*Aᵀ)⁻¹ = 1 / sigmaMin Aᵀ ^ 2` holds
   for every `A` only because both sides are 0 when `AAᵀ` is singular; `tanThetaMax` is 0 exactly at
   angle π/2; `mapAbsCondition` is 0 where `f` is not differentiable. Add the invertibility /
   differentiability hypotheses to the tagged statements. (M5)
6. **"Expected error" is a definition, not an expectation.** `Kaczmarz.expErr`,
   `SketchProject.expErr`, `choleskyExpectedTrace`, `volumeSamplingExpectedFrobSq` are recursions or
   finite sums; for `A = 0` the Kaczmarz "expected error" is 0. Define the law and make the sum a
   lemma (the manifest rebuilds Solvers but not `LowRank/Cholesky` or real volume sampling). (A3)
7. **Names that mislead.** `residual Q A := A - Q * (Qᴴ * A)` is not `b − Ax` and collides with
   Mathlib's root `residual`; `frobInner` is the real part of the Frobenius inner product;
   `IsBestRankApprox` is Frobenius-only; `moorePenroseInverse` is defined by choice, not by the SVD
   formula; `LanczosDecomp` is an `abbrev` of `ArnoldiDecomp` with no symmetry; `choleskyStep` is a
   Gaussian-elimination step; `givens` is the transpose of Golub–Van Loan's rotation. Each is
   defensible; each needs one documenting sentence or a rename. (M §1, A §1.2, M8)
8. **The same object spelled several ways.** Rectangular diagonal ×3 (one positioned by an
   unspecified `equivFin`), Schur complement / elimination step ×4, leading minor ×4, prefix sums of
   sorted values ×3, plane rotations ×3, Schatten norm ×2 (one with an unrestricted real exponent),
   eigenprojectors ×2 with near-identical names, Krylov process objects ×3 (`arnoldi*`,
   `ArnoldiDecomp.*`, `lanczosRecurrence`/`lanczosAlpha`/`lanczosBeta`), optimal-error tails ×6,
   principal-angle tangents ×6 (the only bridge is a `sorry`), random signs ×3 (`Bool`, a custom
   `Sign`, `ℝ`), orthogonal-projector-onto-range (`complexColumnProjector` exists only over ℂ). (M §1, A §1, A §3)
9. **Chebyshev coefficients are defined from a complex extension `F : ℂ → ℂ`**, not from `f`, so
   "the Chebyshev truncation of `f`" depends on a choice; `lanczosRitzValue` is a countable
   rational-coefficient supremum chosen for measurability rather than `λ_max(QᵀAQ)`. (A §1.2–1.3)
10. **Half-generalised factorizations.** `IsLU`/`IsCholesky`/`IsQR` take `[LinearOrder n]` but every
    algorithm and existence theorem is `Fin n` and ℝ; Cholesky uses `IsSymm`, Sylvester uses
    `IsHermitian` on the same objects; CG and Lanczos have no `…Succeeds` predicate and their
    realisation theorems are `sorry`. (M8, A §1.2)

### C.2 Theorem statements

11. **"Oblivious subspace embedding" is concluded five different ways** (success ≥ 1−δ vs failure
    ≤ δ; `IsSubspaceEmbedding` vs a Gram-norm event; `<` vs `≤`; `ENNReal` vs `.toReal` vs `μ.real`;
    leverage sampling over ℂ with norms written out). F3's single conclusion shape fixes this. (A1)
12. **The RSVD and Nyström headlines are paper-named and take the probabilistic step as a
    hypothesis.** `rsvd_main`, `gn_main`, `completion_reduction`, `Assembly.rsvd_explicit` carry the
    headline atlas ids while assuming `hinv : ∫ frobSq (…) = k/(t−k−1) · frobSq S₂`; the unconditional
    theorems have 90-character names. Neighbours also parametrise differently (`t` with `k+2 ≤ t`
    vs `k+p` vs `t, s`). (A2)
13. **The spectral core is not uniform yet.** `Inequalities/Mirsky.lean` is ℝ and `Fin` (and holds
    Bhatia IV.3.4, tagged `lidskii`), von Neumann and the Frobenius Mirsky inequality are ℝ-only,
    `exists_unitary_complement` and the LU/Cholesky/QR existence theorems are `Fin`-indexed, the
    singular-value isometry invariances are ℝ-only. The F1 acceptance grep fails on Mirsky today. (M6)
14. **Ritz, power and random-start results depend on Mathlib's chosen eigenvector basis**
    (`hb : (hA.eigenvectorBasis i₁).ofLp ⬝ᵥ b ≠ 0`, ambiguous when the top eigenvalue repeats), use
    unsorted eigenvalues with an `htop` hypothesis instead of `eigenvalues₀`, and take hypotheses
    that are always true (`hT : (QᵀAQ).IsHermitian`; Weyl's `hAB : (A + B).IsHermitian`). (A5, M §2)
15. **Degree and index conventions drift.** Gauss exactness in both `degree < 2q` and legacy
    `natDegree ≤ 2q−1, 0 < q` forms with downstream results on the legacy one; the unprimed
    `mem_krylovSpace_iff` is the one with a hypothesis; random-start Lanczos counts `K_q` (`2 ≤ q`,
    `(q−1)²`) while power counts matvecs (`2q+1`). (A6)
16. **Bundles carry the atlas tags.** 64 Matrix conjunction theorems are tagged (the largest
    signature is 5.9k characters); 35 atlas results are "proved" only by a `*_properties` bundle
    whose signature is a pasted pretty-print with `_inst_1`/`u_1`; `_fintype` transports reindex by
    `Fintype.equivFin`, now a no-op; one bundle's first and third conjuncts print identically in the
    atlas. Tag the component theorems. (M9, I5, A §2.5)
17. **Constants and forms visibly weaker than the literature**, documented but surprising:
    Hutchinson needs ≈1900 ε⁻² log(2/δ) samples against ≈8 (CK21); SLQ `1024e²`; sparse OSE 1356
    and 306456; JL union-bounds over N² ordered pairs; GMRES's diagonalisable bound covers only
    real-diagonalisable `A`; the Nyström trace bound is stated through a factor `A = FᵀF` instead of
    the eigenvalue tail of `A`. (A10, A §2.5)
18. **Vocabulary leftovers inside statements.** `(B − A).PosSemidef` instead of `≤` in 7 files;
    raw `Pᴴ * P = 1` / `Pᵀ * P = 1` hypotheses in about 40 statements, two of them named
    `_of_hasOrthonormalCols`; `IsSymm` in 35 real signatures against `IsHermitian` in ~240; the
    vector 2-norm spelled three ways (`‖WithLp.toLp 2 x‖`, `√(x ⬝ᵥ x)`, `{x // x ⬝ᵥ x = 1}`);
    snake_case paper names (`rsvd_main`, `jl_lemma`, `hutchinson_of_rademacher`). (M7, M §3, A §3)
19. **Atlas tags on the wrong theorem.** Schur density tagged `schur-triangularization`,
    `IsUpperHessenberg` tagged `givens-rotation-def`, `chebyshevResidual` tagged
    `inverse-polynomial-approx` while its docstring says `chebyshev-minimax`, a `U : Matrix n (Fin 0) ℂ`
    helper tagged `leverage-sampling-ose`, deterministic lemmas tagged `gaussian-ose`. (M §2, A §2.5)

### C.3 Probability and random matrices

P1. **Two random-matrix worlds that never meet.** The Tropp matrix-concentration port is ℂ,
    `Fin d` with `[NeZero d]` (34 statements), `‖·‖` and `.toReal` tails; the Gaussian world is ℝ,
    `specNorm`, `ENNReal`. `lambdaMax`, `matrixExp`, `traceExp`, `intrinsicDimension`,
    `rectSecondMoment`, `cumulantSum` are ℂ-only, so matrix Bernstein/Chernoff cannot be applied to
    any Gaussian or sub-Gaussian matrix in the library. (P1)
P2. **Tails in four currencies**: `(μ {…}).toReal ≤` (22), `μ {…} ≤ ENNReal.ofReal` (34),
    `μ.real {…} ≤` (9), `1 − ofReal ≤ μ {…}` (1); `ChiSquare.lean` uses two for the same χ² tails.
    F2's `μ.real` rule covers the touched files only. (P2)
P3. **"Standard Gaussian" is spelled six ways**: `HasLaw X (gaussianMatrix m n) μ`,
    `μ.map X = gaussianMatrix n t` (F2 in flight), entrywise `IsStandardGaussian` + `iIndepFun`
    (29 sites in Estimation), `Measure.pi fun _ => gaussianReal 0 1` (277 occurrences in 40 files),
    Mathlib's `stdGaussian (EuclideanSpace …)` (8 files), `HasGaussianLaw`. There is no
    `gaussianVector` and no bridge between `Measure.pi` and `stdGaussian`. (P3)
P4. **Independent Rademacher signs are spelled three ways**, and noncommutative Khintchine is
    proved twice under the same atlas id. (P4)
P5. **A disjunctive hypothesis** `(∀ k, IsStandardGaussian μ (g k)) ∨ (∀ k, IsRademacher μ (g k))`
    in 5 theorems whose proofs only use a sub-Gaussian MGF and unit variance. (P5)
P6. **Headlines are `let`–`∧` bundles** (41 tagged; `matrix_chernoff` has five conjuncts and three
    `let`s, the first conjunct a bookkeeping identity); `intrinsic_matrix_bernstein_expectation` is
    the library's only `∃ C` statement, with `C = 20` in the proof. (P6)
P7. **Law abbreviations against Mathlib's order** (`IsStandardGaussian μ g` vs `HasLaw g ν μ`),
    and consumers keep binders the law already gives (`AEMeasurable`, `Measurable`,
    `[IsProbabilityMeasure μ]`, inconsistently between the Gaussian and Rademacher twins). (P7)
P8. **Junk-valued spectral vocabulary drives hypotheses**: `intrinsicDimension 0 = 0` forces
    `hM : M ≠ 0`; `rectSecondMoment` is 0 for non-integrable `Z`; PSD is `0 ≤ lambdaMin ∧ lambdaMax ≤ L`
    in one Chernoff and `PosSemidef` in the other; a.e.-Hermitian hypotheses in 54 places. (P8)
P9. **Hanson–Wright exists twice**: the clean tagged `hanson_wright_mgf` and an untagged `Fin n`
    port with four constant hypotheses, HighDimProb binder names at 69 sites and `maxRecDepth 5000`.
    Two public `_fin` twins carry the same atlas id as their general forms (ChiSquare, McDiarmid). (P9)
P10. **Provenance labels** ("operator re-derivation `sh:…`" on 133 lines, "actual" on 138) and
    NLAlib-specific subtrees inside ForMathlib (`UniformSampling*`, 641 lines, "supports srht-ose";
    five weak-density files, 1027 lines, only for `wishart-lambda-min-tail`). (P10)
